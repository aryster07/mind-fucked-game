Online Mind Trick: Multiplayer Gameplay Scenario

Game Overview

Players: 2 to 6 Players (Real-time).

Deck: 1 Standard Deck (2-4 players) or 2 Decks mixed (5-6 players).

Turn Timer: 15 seconds per turn to keep the pace fast.

Phase 1: The Lobby & The Deal

Waiting Room: Players join a lobby. The host starts the game once at least 2 players are ready.

The Deal: Every player is dealt 4 cards face-down.

Synchronized Peek: A countdown appears: "3... 2... 1... Memorize!"

Only YOUR cards flip face-up on your screen (opponents' cards stay face-down as "?").

You have exactly 8 seconds to memorize your own 4 cards.

Blackout: YOUR cards flip face-down. The game begins.

Phase 2: The Gameplay Loop (Turn-Based)

The turn order moves clockwise. When it is Player A's turn:

Throw (Action):

Player A selects one card from their hand to discard.

Visual: The card flies to the central Discard Pile, landing face-up for everyone to see.

Strategy: If Player A throws a high card (e.g., 10), it's a safe play. If they throw a Power Card (7, 9, 11, 13), a special event triggers immediately after the replacement.

Replace (Automatic):

The server automatically deals the top card from the Deck to Player A's empty slot.

Private Reveal: Only Player A sees this new card for 3 seconds. To all other players, it remains face-down.

Power-Up Alert: If the drawn card is a Power Card (7, 9, J, or K), a popup appears showing:
  - The power card you drew
  - What the power does
  - Reminder: "You can use this power when you throw this card!"

Memory Test: Player A must memorize this new value before it flips back over.

Phase 3: Multiplayer Power-Ups

If Player A throws a Power Card, a popup reminder appears showing:
  - The power that was activated
  - Instructions on what to do next
  - Available actions

Then the interaction phase begins.

7 (The Refresh):

Effect: Player A's hand flips face-up only on their screen for 3 seconds.

Bonus: Player A can drag and reorder their cards to different positions during this time.

Others see: An icon indicating Player A is refreshing their memory.

9 (The Blind Swap):

Effect: Player A's UI highlights all other players (Player B, C, D, etc.).

Action: Player A clicks one of their own cards, then clicks Target Player (e.g., Player C).

Result: The server instantly swaps the two cards. Neither player sees the face value during the swap. Player A now has Player C's card, and vice versa.

J / 11 (The Chaos Shuffle):

Effect: Player A selects a Target Player (e.g., Player D).

Result: PlaySpy):

Effect: Player A selects a Target Player (e.g., Player B).

Result: Player B's hand is revealed face-up ONLY to Player A for 3 seconds (private view).

Others see: An icon indicating Player A is spying on Player B, but they can't see the cards.

Strategic: Only Player A gains the knowledge of Player B's hand

Social Aspect: Everyone now knows Player B's score, making them a target for Swaps if they have low cards.

Phase 4: The Endgame (The "Show" Call)

Objective: Be the earliest to reveal a hand sum of 10 or less.

The Call (Instant Action):

At the start of their turn (before throwing), Player A can click the "SHOW" button if they believe their hand sum is ≤ 10.

The game pauses immediately. No one else takes a turn.

The Resolution (Win or Lose):

Player A's cards are flipped face-up for validation.

Scenario A: TRUE CALL (Sum ≤ 10)

Result: Player A WINS THE GAME INSTANTLY.

The game ends. Player A takes the pot.

Scenario B: FALSE CALL (Sum > 10)

Result: Player A LOSES INSTANTLY (Busted).

Player A is disqualified or receives the maximum penalty score.

The winner is determined by finding the player with the lowest score among the remaining players (or the game continues without Player A, depending on house rules).
