## How to Play with Friends

### Create a Room
1. Open https://mindcooked.web.app
2. Enter your name
3. Click "🌐 Create Room"
4. Share the 6-character room code with friends

### Join a Room
1. Open https://mindcooked.web.app
2. Enter your name
3. Click "🚪 Join Room"
4. Enter the room code
5. Wait for host to start the game

### Solo Play
1. Open https://mindcooked.web.app
2. Click "🎮 Solo Play"
3. Play against 3 bots
